HOW TO GET YOUR ADMIN APK
=========================
1. Open ADMIN_URL.txt and replace  https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/admin.php
   with the real address of your admin page (the one you open in Chrome).
2. Create a new repository on github.com (Add file > Upload files) and upload
   EVERYTHING in this folder, including the hidden ".github" folder.
   (Easiest: unzip, then drag all the files/folders into GitHub.)
3. Click the "Actions" tab. The "Build Android APK" job starts by itself
   (or choose it and press "Run workflow"). Wait ~3-5 minutes for the green tick.
4. Open the finished run, scroll to "Artifacts", download
   "Fohow-Zodiac-Admin-APK", unzip it, and send app-debug.apk to your phone.
5. On the phone open the APK, allow "Install unknown apps" when asked, install.
   Tap "Zodiac Admin" - it opens your admin sign-in page directly.

To change the address later: edit ADMIN_URL.txt on GitHub and commit; a new APK is built.
